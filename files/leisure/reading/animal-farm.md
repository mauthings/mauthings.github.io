> When Boxer heard this he fetched the small straw hat which he wore in summer to keep the flies out of his ears, and flung it on to the fire with the rest.
>
> **Note:** can indicate from this that the euphoric chase for "animalism" is way too far. abandoning useful things just because a single sentence from leader is irrational.

> and when they came back in the evening it was noticed that the milk had disappeared.
>
> **Note:** Maybe it was drunk by Napoleon.

### [Green Underline]

> Milk and apples (this has been proved by Science, comrades) contain substances absolutely necessary to the well-being of a pig. We pigs are brainworkers.
>
> **Note:** That's excuse! Brain labor does NOT transcend physical labor at all. The leaders took the harvests in the name of equality, and that's nothing different from salvery. And even worse, this betrayed the so-called "comrades".
