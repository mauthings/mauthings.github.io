## 摘录与批注 / Excerpts and Annotations

### [Green Underline]

> For Kant, pure apperception is an act of spontaneity and as such is different from sensibility, which is a passive receptivity for sensible intuitions.

### [Green Underline]

> My object is to persuade all those who think metaphysics worth studying that it is absolutely necessary to pause a moment and, disregarding all that has been done, to propose first the preliminary question, “Whether such a thing as metaphysics be at all possible?”
>
> **Note:** Critique of Pure Reason: 形而上学，它的方法在开始时是独断的，也就是不预先检验理性是否有能力从事这样一项庞大的计划，就深信不疑地承担了这项施工。

### [Green Underline]

> Human reason so delights in constructions that it has several times built up a tower and then razed it to examine the nature of the foundation.
>
> **Note:** 人类在构筑了庞大的理性大厦之后，才反应过来去检查它的地基是否稳固。数学、物理学、生化等等一系列科学都是如此，更不要提哲学了

### [Green Underline]

> Some, in the proud consciousness of their possessions, which are ancient and therefore considered legitimate, will take their metaphysical compendia in their hands and look down on him with contempt;
>
> **Note:** 坚持原有形而上学观点的人对于康德的批判哲学嗤之以鼻，因为康德对理性本身是否可行的doubt冒犯了他们，而他们对自己由来已久的学说又信心满满

### [Green Underline]

> he must confess that a radical reform, or rather a rebirth of the science according to a new plan, is unavoidable, however much men may struggle against it for a while.
>
> **Note:** 哥白尼式的革命

### [Green Underline]

> This is as much as to say that there is not, and cannot be, any such thing as metaphysics at all.
>
> **Note:** 休谟提出的“怀疑论”摧毁了因果必然性与形而上学，把自我看作一束知觉。
